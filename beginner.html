A Few Taigi Greetings
  • 你好
    Tâi-lô: Lí-hó  ·  POJ: Lí-hó
    Hello
  • 你食飽未？
    Tâi-lô: Lí tsia̍h-pá-buē?  ·  POJ: Lí chia̍h-pá-bōe?
    Have you eaten yet? — used the way English uses "how are you"
  • 你有閒無？
    Tâi-lô: Lí ū-îng-bô?  ·  POJ: Lí ū-êng-bô?
    Are you free? Got a minute?
  • 多謝
    Tâi-lô: To-siā  ·  POJ: To-siā
    Thank you
  • 歹勢
    Tâi-lô: Pháinn-sè  ·  POJ: Pháiⁿ-sè
    Sorry / excuse me
  • 真好食
    Tâi-lô: Tsin hó-tsia̍h  ·  POJ: Chin hó-chia̍h
    This is delicious — useful, given the snacks

Tâi-lô is the current Ministry of Education standard; Pe̍h-ōe-jī (POJ) is the older missionary system still used in writing today.
